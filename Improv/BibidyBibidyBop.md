Description: |-
  Circle game with monkey in the middle.  The Monkey steps in front of player and declares the game.  If the player fails, they become the monkey:
   bibidybibidybop - player must say bop before the monkey is complete.
  Bop - player must say nothing
  christmas tree - arms above head, and neighbors are twinkling.
  charlie's angels - Middle makes and X shape, right is gun, left is HT.
  menora - fingers up wrists together, neighbors light the menora.
  airplane - make goggle eyes, neighbors are wings.
  boat - rowing, and neighbors are the boat
  jello - wiggle and neighbors are the bowl.
  Elephant - make a trunk, then neighbors are ears.
